Scope Notes

"Microsomia" is not standard fetal-medicine terminology — undergrowth is described as SGA (a size descriptor) or FGR (a pathological process); the two are not synonymous, and distinguishing them needs serial growth and Doppler, not a single EFW. UA-PI here is shown against the INTERGROWTH-21st population mean, not a full percentile band, since that requires the SD model — treat departures qualitatively and prioritise AEDF/REDF, which are absolute findings. CPR <1.0 (MCA-PI < UA-PI) is the standard qualitative brain-sparing flag. Kidney paediatric reference is a population mean (for comparison), not an upper-limit cutoff like the other age-graded tables. Hepatic artery is assessed here by resistive index, not diameter, since it has no standard reference calibre the way the portal/splenic/hepatic veins do. Bladder wall thickness depends on distension — always re-check on a distended bladder before calling a thickened wall abnormal. Endometrial thickness is context-dependent (menopausal status, symptoms, cycle phase) — the tool asks which context applies rather than using one fixed number. Uterine artery PI is shown against a fixed 95th-centile figure, not a MoM adjusted for maternal ethnicity, weight, or height, which is the more precise approach where available. Ovarian cysts get two separate assessments: a quick size/follow-up-band descriptor, and the full IOTA Simple Rules (all 10 features, the published decision algorithm). The ADNEX model is deliberately not implemented — it's a multi-coefficient logistic regression requiring a serum CA-125 value and precise published coefficients across five outcome categories, and a hand-built version of that carries more risk of a subtle, hard-to-catch error than the benefit justifies here. For an inconclusive Simple Rules result, or for a formal percentage risk, use expert ultrasound assessment or the official ADNEX calculator directly. Postmenopausal ovarian volume has no single agreed cutoff in the literature and none is asserted here. Patient Log is entirely local to this device (IndexedDB) and off by default — nothing is saved unless the consent checkbox is ticked for that specific episode, and every episode can be deleted individually or all at once. There is no account, no sync, and no network call anywhere in this app; closing the browser tab discards anything not explicitly saved. PSA density: prostatitis can raise PSA disproportionately to volume; on a 5-alpha-reductase inhibitor, doubling measured PSA before this calculation is the common convention, since these drugs suppress PSA production independent of cancer status.
